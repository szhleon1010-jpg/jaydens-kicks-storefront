# Jayden’s Kicks Studio

Open index.html directly to preview the storefront. For Vercel, select jaydens-kicks as the project root. The included config serves the files as-is with no build or install.

The catalog uses sample USD prices, made-to-order availability, production estimates, and generated design mockups. Verify these before publishing. Production estimates exclude shipping.

The bag stays in the visitor’s browser. Checkout generates a downloadable text request and records a payment preference only. It does not send requests, confirm stock, or process payments. To go live, configure a real order destination and a hosted checkout or invoice workflow with a payment provider. Do not collect card numbers in the static site.

The built-in image generator made sneakers.png as a four-panel product sheet: a white koi and Japanese-wave sneaker in cobalt campaign and powder-blue catalog shots, a black violet-nebula sneaker with stars on lavender, and a black electric-blue flame sneaker on pale mint.

The catalog now has six product detail pages linked from the gallery. The custom request form downloads a draft, and reviews are stored in this browser and shown on the matching product page. Shared reviews and online request delivery require a connected service.

The built-in image generator created studio-pairs.png from a three-panel product photography brief: a white sneaker with jade botanical vines on sage, a cream sneaker with orange and plum sunset art on peach, and a charcoal sneaker with silver lightning and teal traces on gray-blue.
